# Free KDP & Book Templates Directory

Free templates for self-publishing on Amazon KDP and beyond. All links point to official platform or publisher pages. No affiliate links.

## Interior layouts

### KDP Interior Templates (Print & Paperback)
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Amazon KDP
- Official: https://kdp.amazon.com/en/help/topic/G202061670
- Steps:
  1. Go to the KDP Help interior templates page and pick your trim size (e.g. 6 x 9 in).
  2. Download the matching Word (.docx) template file.
  3. Paste your manuscript into the template, applying the built-in heading styles.
  4. Export the finished file as a print-ready PDF.
  5. Upload the PDF interior to your KDP paperback or hardcover title.

### Kindle Create
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Amazon Kindle Create (desktop app)
- Official: https://kdp.amazon.com/en/help/topic/G202013730
- Steps:
  1. Download and install Kindle Create for Windows or macOS.
  2. Create a new project and import your Word (.docx) or plain-text manuscript.
  3. Apply chapter and section formatting using the built-in themes.
  4. Preview the ebook on Kindle devices and tablets in the built-in previewer.
  5. Export a KPF file and upload it to your KDP ebook title.

### Reedsy Book Editor - Free Interior Templates
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Reedsy (web)
- Official: https://reedsy.com/book-editing
- Steps:
  1. Sign up for a free Reedsy account and open the Book Editor.
  2. Create a new book and choose a trim size and theme.
  3. Write or paste your chapters; the editor applies professional typesetting automatically.
  4. Export a print-ready PDF for KDP, Lulu, or IngramSpark, or an EPUB for ebook stores.
  5. Download and upload the exported files to your publishing platform.

### Google Docs Manuscript Template
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Google Docs (web)
- Official: https://docs.google.com/
- Steps:
  1. Open Google Docs and start a new document from the 'Manuscript' template in the gallery.
  2. Set page size to 6 x 9 inches (File > Page setup) and margins to at least 0.75 in.
  3. Apply Heading 1 to chapter titles so they export cleanly to Word or PDF.
  4. Write or paste your manuscript, then download as .docx or PDF.
  5. Import the file into Kindle Create or upload directly to KDP as needed.

### KDP eBook Manuscript Formatting Guide
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Amazon KDP
- Official: https://kdp.amazon.com/en/help/topic/G202013730
- Steps:
  1. Open the KDP eBook manuscript formatting help article.
  2. Follow the checklist: clean styles, page breaks, front matter, and image sizing.
  3. Format your Word document using the recommended heading and paragraph styles.
  4. Save as filtered HTML or .docx and preview with Kindle Previewer.
  5. Upload the final file to your KDP ebook bookshelf.

### Lulu Print Book Templates
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Lulu
- Official: https://www.lulu.com/templates
- Steps:
  1. Visit the Lulu templates page and select your book size and binding type.
  2. Download the Word or InDesign template file for your layout.
  3. Format your manuscript inside the template with the preset margins and gutter.
  4. Save/export the interior as a PDF and upload it to a new Lulu project.
  5. Review the digital proof before ordering or publishing.

## Cover templates

### KDP Cover Templates (Cover Calculator)
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Amazon KDP
- Official: https://kdp.amazon.com/en/help/topic/G202013750
- Steps:
  1. Open the KDP Cover Calculator and enter your trim size, page count, and paper type.
  2. Click Calculate to get the exact cover spread dimensions and spine width.
  3. Download the blank cover template as a PNG or PDF.
  4. Open the template in your design tool and place front cover, spine, and back cover art within the safe zones.
  5. Export the flattened cover as a print-ready PDF and upload it to KDP.

### Reedsy Book Cover Templates
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Reedsy (web)
- Official: https://reedsy.com/design/book-cover-templates
- Steps:
  1. Browse the free Reedsy cover template gallery by genre.
  2. Select a template and open it in the Reedsy cover creator.
  3. Replace placeholder text with your title, subtitle, and author name.
  4. Swap in your own artwork or choose from the free image library.
  5. Export the finished cover and upload it to KDP or your chosen printer.

### Canva Free Book Templates
- Cost: Free (free tier)
- Platform: Canva (web)
- Official: https://www.canva.com/templates/book-covers/
- Steps:
  1. Open Canva and search for 'book cover' or 'KDP cover' templates.
  2. Pick a free template marked with no crown icon (free-tier assets only).
  3. Edit the title, author name, and imagery using Canva's editor.
  4. Download as PDF Print for KDP cover upload, or PNG for ebook use.
  5. Confirm final dimensions match your KDP cover calculator output before uploading.

### IngramSpark Cover & Interior Templates
- Cost: Free
- Platform: IngramSpark
- Official: https://www.ingramspark.com/templates
- Steps:
  1. Log in to a free IngramSpark account and open the template downloads page.
  2. Enter your trim size, page count, and binding to generate template specs.
  3. Download the PDF cover template and the interior template file.
  4. Design your cover and interior within the provided bleed and safe-zone guides.
  5. Upload print-ready PDFs when setting up your IngramSpark title.

## Copyright pages

### KDP Copyright Page Templates
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Amazon KDP / Word
- Official: https://kdp.amazon.com/en/help/topic/G202013730
- Steps:
  1. Open the KDP copyright page help article and copy the sample text block.
  2. Paste the block onto a new page after your title page in Word.
  3. Replace the placeholder ISBN, publication year, and author/publisher name with your own.
  4. Keep the 'All rights reserved' line and optional printing/edition notes as needed.
  5. Export the manuscript as PDF with the copyright page included.

## Series bibles

### Notion Series Bible Template
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Notion (free plan)
- Official: https://www.notion.so/
- Steps:
  1. Create a free Notion account and open a new blank page.
  2. Add databases for Characters, Locations, Timeline, and Plot threads.
  3. Link related entries (e.g. a character to the scenes they appear in).
  4. Use Notion's free template gallery or duplicate a community series bible template.
  5. Duplicate the page for each book in your series and keep a master world-building hub.

### Series Bible Spreadsheet Template
- Cost: Free
- Platform: Google Sheets / Excel
- Official: https://www.google.com/sheets/about/
- Steps:
  1. Create a new spreadsheet with tabs for Characters, Locations, Timeline, and Series Arc.
  2. List each character's name, role, appearance, and book of first appearance.
  3. Record location names, settings, and which books feature them.
  4. Enter a chronological timeline of series events across books.
  5. Save a copy per series and update it as you publish new books.
